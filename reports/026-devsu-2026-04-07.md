## F) Interview Prep

### STAR+R Stories

1. **Hands-on dev + PM combination** — *PULSE:* Built full-stack PWA (React 18/TS/Node/Express/PostgreSQL) while also managing the product roadmap, user feedback, and payment integration with PayPhone. Result: $2,500+ first-week revenue, 100+ users, 125+ transactions. *Reflection:* Being able to code gives me empathy for engineering estimates and technical trade-offs that pure PMs miss.

2. **Client delivery at consulting scale** — *Eagle Eye:* Ran drone videography consultancy with 50+ clients, managing expectations, timelines, and quality across 15+ remote contractors with 2-4 day turnaround. Result: Sustainable business with repeat clients. *Reflection:* In consulting, underpromise and overdeliver is the only sustainable strategy.

3. **Complex multi-stakeholder project delivery** — *Karpowership:* 4 Powership installations, 25+ contractors, international stakeholders (Turkey, Ecuador government), high-pressure energy crisis context. Result: 90% commute reduction. *Reflection:* The PM's job in complex projects is to be the information router — making sure the right people have the right information at the right time.

4. **Risk management affecting scope, schedule, quality** — *PULSE:* 26-item security audit identified risks in authentication, payment handling, XSS, CSRF. Managed scope to address all before launch without delaying timeline. *Reflection:* Risk management is about ranking — not all risks are equal, and the PM must prioritize ruthlessly.

5. **Scaling programs and monitoring KPIs** — *Radioi99:* Scaled from 1 to 8+ marketing programs, tracking audience growth metrics. Achieved 25% audience growth. *Reflection:* KPIs only matter if they drive decisions — vanity metrics waste everyone's time.

6. **Agile/DevOps in practice** — *FireApp:* Worked in 5-person team on frontend development (Webflow/Angular 9). Participated in sprint planning, daily standups, code reviews. *Reflection:* Agile works when the team buys in; imposed Agile creates bureaucracy.

7. **Remote team communication** — *Eagle Eye + Karpowership:* Both roles required managing distributed teams across time zones. Built communication cadences (daily standups, weekly reviews, async updates) that worked for remote-first environments. *Reflection:* Remote communication requires 3x the documentation of in-person work.

8. **Procurement and resource management** — *Offshore Gas & Oil:* Managed procurement logistics for 5+ remote sites, optimizing supply chains and resource allocation. Result: 20% productivity improvement. *Reflection:* Resource management is a PM skill that transfers across industries — whether it's people, equipment, or cloud resources.
